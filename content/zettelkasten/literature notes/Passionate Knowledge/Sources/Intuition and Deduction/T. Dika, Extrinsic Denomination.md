Sources Connecting Regulae with Aquinas: Tarek R. Dika, Extrinsic Denomination and the Origins of Early Modern Metaphysics:

Interesting connection relating, not to intuition, but instead the scholastic idea of scientific _habitus_.

Dika points out that “Descartes’s method creates, arguably for the first time, a chasm between how things can be known by the intellect and how they are in reality—i.e., between “epistemology” and “ontology”—that motivates him to pursue metaphysics after the Regulae”, and also that “[Descartes] experienced firsthand the mechanization of natural philosophy in the late 1610s through the Dutch natural philosopher Isaac Beeckman.” However, his analysis stops here. Probably not worth any sort of official mention, and not worth reading, but thought it would be interesting.
