To be precise, he wanted the world to destroy him as a matter of course. With a glance. disappearing as simply as he appeared. A fast twitch without trace. Clean dust. A steep drop off a cliff edge, light filling an empty silhoutte. The teacup left half finished. 

He pranced down the street. An ambulance passed by. Swiftly followed by two dancing police cars. Ligaments of life employed only to remind that the world is there with, or without, you. Ethan felt his sinews extending down to his ankles. Soft muscle holding earth - A sense that nothing in this street held a soul. Trespassing freely until his own body felt foreign. Since his teenage years he knew he had died endlessly, like a prayer, remade in an image unlike his own. Nobody occupies his flesh. 

Everytime he went outside it was like this, bright, nauseating lights. Hypersaturated and unbecoming, he wished he could be like the bile that exited his body. Sticking brown liquid furnishing his life. Cloying and claustrophobic - the outside world struck an imposing figure. In conversation he realized, briefly and certainly, that he had no business being anywhere. He retreated inside, his room. Becoming a marshall of his own life, drawing lines and scattering small notes. 

There were certain nights, leaving the Goldman office, Ethan lay awake in bed until golden light. His first and final wife - Nadya, placed like an alter beside him. Everything felt quite simple. Everyone knew, through eavesdropping and everlasting whispering. The soft humdrum of banality. She allowed his life to continue.
