James? I'm sorry to be calling you up like this but I hadn't heard your voice in a while., you know. Everyone is kind of missing you, even aunty L believe it or not. How are you? How are things over there back at the old house, did you manage to repaint it. Hell of a job - but call me back and let me know. You can also call A. if I'm not picking up. In case you don't call I'll just tell you it's been good here, Maddie's a little sick, but she'll get over it. Mom died. A. got that new job we were telling you about. You better call soon, love you. As you know, that period of time between my birth and my death was spent in the same house. Mother had often talked of moving into someplace larger, in a better area, maybe even with a front yard. She brought it up daily, but then she died so I no longer had to go anywhere. I never remember moving in, so I like to tell myself I was born here. This is a lie. We moved when I was four from a small place to this even smaller place. You've heard the story: I ran into the house excited and slouched back frown-faced asking "where's the rest of the house." When you're a child you assume everything tends upward. This was all before father left, so I like to tell myself I was born without him too. 

By the way: mother died. I slipped it in earlier, but I guess I should tell you properly. So here it is: Mother is dead. I assume this is important information for you, hence this letter. This would be the part of the letter where I tell you when and where all the future arrangements concerning her corpse happen. Nothing like that is happening. That might be the part of the letter you cite when you eventually call me some spattering of stupidity and selfishness. Let's save us both the trouble. I was the one who took care of her, so I get to decide. Not you, not father, not even her. Life was something that was taken from me, and everybody somehow feels like it's right now, at this moment, the exact perfect time they can enter and make the Decisions. 

It's very easy to make things up in here, but it's a horrible habit, so I try to avoid it whenever I can.
