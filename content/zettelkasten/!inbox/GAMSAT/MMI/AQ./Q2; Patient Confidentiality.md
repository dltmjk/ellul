A 23 year old woman undergoes a termination of pregnancy under general anaesthesia. Unfortunately, the patient had a complication during surgery and her care needed to be transferred to the intensive care unit.

The patient’s spouse/partner would like to know the nature of the procedure and the reason for the surgery. The patient has expressed her wishes preoperatively not to disclose her pregnancy and the procedure to her family members.

In the next station, you will discuss these issues with the examiner and explain your opinion regarding the scenario.

Framework: STAR

S:
Spouse wants to know
Woman doesn't want anybody to know

Talk about the tension here, the main tension is if it is right for doctors to disclose personal information to family members, even if they are stressed, and how would I handle this situation given these conditions. 

T:
===Talk about the values more===
Cons for breaching confidentiality:
Reduced trust in doctors generally and especially from women who seek care in an abortion clinic, or for other private matters. 
I need to maintain patient doctor confidentiality 

However there are some pros: 
Family members are quite worried
Discussions with those close might help in clearing any complications, especially if they can offer information regarding past medical history 

A:
As a medical professional I need to communicate the patients wishes clearly to the spouse and relay all other important information, such as the seriousness of the complication and what the ICU will entail.

I will do this in an empathetic fashion and listen to any concerns while maintaining patient doctor confidentiality. 

We are doing everything to their wishes and at our best ability.
