You are a registrar at a metropolitan Hospital. On a busy Saturday night near the end of a long shift, a young couple arrive at the emergency where the young lady looks visibly bruised with cuts. The young man also looked visibly shaken up. When treating them, they are asked about their whereabouts and they both state that they were in a car accident. You are suspicious about the situation. How do you handle the situation?

Framework: RADAR

R: Routinely Screen
Take history normally, don't single out anything yet 

A: Ask Directly in Private
If the injury is inconsistent with the stated mechanism, then this is a red flag. 
I would separate the people, saying that it is usual hospital protocol. 

D: Document Carefully
I'd start by asking non judgemental open questions, relating to the injury, such as. I notice you have a number of bruises, did someone do this to you? 

Assess Safety: 
See whether it's safe for her to return home, what the family situation is, whether children are involved

Refer Respond:
Give referrals to different legal groups.

Otherwise: if she discloses nothing, I'd leave the door open and not pressure her since disclosure has to be on her terms for her safety.
