It’s 5:30 pm and you still have 30 minutes left of your 12 hour shift at the hospital. Results for one of your patients is back and it shows that they are suffering from early stage skin cancer. How would you relay this information to your anxious patient?

What considerations would you have to take into account? How would your actions change if you had read these results and your shift was supposed to have finished already 30 minutes ago?

Framework Used: [[STAR]] & [[SPIKES]]

STAR:
Quick recap leading into values 

T:
Values, Patient wants to know. I am tired and may not be in the best condition to tell them. I would like to relay this information as best as possible for the patients sake. I would try to see, if I am too tired, if there is anybody else who could either be with me and support me. If it is exceptionally hard for me, then I would see if another person could relay this information who is in a better condition. 

A:
Using SPIKES to Describe how I would relay this: 

Setting:
I would make sure the patient and any family members are in a private setting

P,Invitation:
I would start by asking if this is a good time to share the results of the scan, or test. 

Knowledge,
I would communicate the findings and relevance in a clear and concise manner, not going into the details for now and what is happening

Empathy,
I would say x, what is x? 

Strategy,
What are the next steps forward. 

R:
Keeping this in mind I hope that the result is a situation in which this important information is relayed well and with empathy for the patient.
