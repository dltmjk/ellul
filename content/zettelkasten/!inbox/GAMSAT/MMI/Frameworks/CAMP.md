CAMP Structure:
Questions relating to the role of doctors: 
	What do you think doctors do other than treating patients?  
	Where do you see yourself in 10 years’ time?  
	What are you hoping to achieve in your medical career?  
	What challenges do you think you will face in Medicine?  
	What are the pros and cons of being a doctor?

Clinical 
Patient Care: Meetings, reading journals, attending courses

Academic 
Teaching and Research

Management 
Managing others, project running, and developing services, quality improvement 

Personal
Personal attributes, Hobbies. They have their own life to live
