History Taking:

**Calgary-Cambridge Model** — the broader consultation structure ICE sits inside: Initiating → Gathering Information → Physical Exam → Explanation & Planning → Closing

**SOCRATES** — mnemonic for characterising a symptom like pain (Site, Onset, Character, Radiation, Associations, Timing, Exacerbating/relieving factors, Severity)

**For breaking bad news / sensitive disclosure scenarios**

**SPIKES** (Setting, Perception, Invitation, Knowledge, Emotions, Strategy/Summary) — the standard protocol, very MMI-relevant since "breaking bad news" role-plays are common

**NURSE** (Name, Understand, Respect, Support, Explore) — specifically for responding to a patient's emotion once it surfaces, often used alongside SPIKES
