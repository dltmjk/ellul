**SPIKES** (Setting, Perception, Invitation, Knowledge, Emotions, Strategy/Summary) — the standard protocol, very MMI-relevant since "breaking bad news" role-plays are common

Setting:
The patient should only be provided with the bad news when they are in a private setting that contains little to no distractions to ensure the message is being properly received. Examples of inappropriate settings are: via phone call or in an open area such as a hallway. Furthermore, the clinician should remember to be facing the patient and the family throughout the process

Perception:
Have no idea what this entails? 

Invitation:
Ask them if it is ok for you to show them to results of the scan, or any other medical documents which relate to their situation. 

Is this a good time to share with you what I believe is going on?" allow for the patient to decide if they are ready to proceed

Knowledge:
Speak Slowly
Maintaining eye contact 
Be clear and concise with my language and what is happening

Empathy:
.

Strategy:
What is going to happen next.
