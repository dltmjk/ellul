Content areas likely to be examined

Melbourne's MMI regularly touches on:

- **Rural health** — access/workforce shortages, telehealth, "why doctors don't stay rural," rural pathway policy debates
- **Indigenous health** — the "Closing the Gap" framework is the single most useful thing to read up on (life expectancy gap, cultural safety, historical context of mistrust in the health system, community-controlled health organisations)
- **Ethical dilemmas** — confidentiality, consent, resource allocation, conscientious objection, euthanasia/assisted dying (topical in Victoria specifically, since VAD is legal there)
- **Current healthcare system literacy** — Medicare, the PBS, private vs public health, healthcare funding debates
- **Personal insight** — motivations, strengths/weaknesses, handling conflict/failure, teamwork

For Indigenous health specifically, the **Closing the Gap** portal is the best single overview (background, current national agreement, key statistics), and it's worth a read regardless of which uni you're aiming for.
