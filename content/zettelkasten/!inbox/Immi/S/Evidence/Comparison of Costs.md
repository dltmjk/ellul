# Comparison of Costs
Tuition in Hong Kong (18. HKU Tuition Fees Webpage):
Undergraduate Degree in Bioscience Related Field:
49,500 * 3 
= 148500 HKD

GET MBBS Degree:
49,500 * 4
= 148500 HKD 

Undergraduate Degree + GET MBBS Degree: 
= 297000 HKD (55,300 AUD)

Tuition in Australia (4e. Doctor of Medicine CoE): 
402,000 AUD over four years. 

While tuition in Hong Kong is lower, the Hong Kong pathway would require seven years of study compared with four in Australia, delaying my entry into the workforce by three years. The true cost of each pathway must therefore include the income forgone during this delay.

In the short term, three years of lost earnings as a junior doctor amount to approximately HK$2.77 million (AUD\$ 505,000) (19a. HK Career Page Detailing Intern and Resident Doctor Pay). This brings the total cost of the Hong Kong pathway to approximately AUD\$ 560,000, which is more than the cost of the Australian pathway. Pay for a Hong Kong Hospital Authority Doctor:

Over the long term, the difference is larger. Because a later start shifts my entire career back by three years, the years ultimately lost are at the end of my career, at senior salary levels, which are far higher than junior salaries. This alone makes the higher tuition in Australia worthwhile. Graduating earlier also brings non-financial advantages, including earlier entry into specialist training and a better chance of entering more competitive specialities. This is particularly valuable given the ease with which Australian-trained doctors can become licensed in Hong Kong.
