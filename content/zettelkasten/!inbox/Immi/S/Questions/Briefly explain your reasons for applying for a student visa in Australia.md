Question 5 (150 Words) - This question is only applicable to applicants with a previous student visa or applying from a non-student visa. Briefly explain your reasons for applying for a student visa in Australia at this time. In your answer, you must demonstrate your level of engagement in the previous course by explaining:

• how you met the course progression requirements;
• participated in assessments, placements etc; and
• completed the course on schedule.

Where you have not completed the course, you must explain your reasons for failing to complete that course and why transferring is a logical progression. You must also provide an explanation for any study gaps longer than 2 months. Non-student visa holders - you must explain how switching to a student visa to study at Curtin University is a logical progression from your current visa.
