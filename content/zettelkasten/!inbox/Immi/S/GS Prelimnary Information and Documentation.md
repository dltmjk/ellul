===For any incomplete study, please explain why here===

TO ASK: Is this incomplete study? 

I began my studies in 2022 at the University of Sydney for a Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Advanced Studies. In short, I switched from my first degree to my second due because I felt that I would have better job prospects with a bachelor of science. Both degrees share the same majors and unit of study tables, and all of my completed units were credited to the new degree. Therefore, there was no impeded course progression. The main difference between the two courses was the mandatory unit, which I made up in an intensive session. 

The following explanation goes into greater detail:
This first part of this degree, the 'Bachelor of Arts'  allows me to choose any major that my department offers (in this case the Arts department), I decided to choose philosophy. 

The second part of the degree, the 'Bachelor of Advanced Studies' allows me to choose a major from any discipline offered by the university. For example, I can study a major that is on offer by either the arts, science of commerce department. For this part of my degree, I chose to major in Software Development.. Therefore, the degree gave me a double major of Philosophy and Software Development. 

I would have to take 48 credit points per year, with both majors having an equal split of credit points. To progress through the degree I would have to pass the mandatory units set out in the Majors respective 'Unit of Study Table'

In 2023, I felt it would be best for my future job opportunities if I graduated with a Bachelor of Science and a Bachelor of Advanced Studies (BSc/BAdvSt) instead of a Bachelor of Arts and a Bachelor of Advanced Studies (BA/BAdvSt). This is because, when applying for software-related roles, I felt it would be better to highlight the Software Development major if it was placed under a Bachelor of Science. 

Given that switching from one degree to the other would also not hinder my course completion date, I switched over. My course completion was not impeded because the BSc/BAdvSt matches the structure of my previous degree exactly. In the BA/BAdvSt, students must select one major from the Table A Subject Areas offered by the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (e.g. Philosophy), and a second major or program from Table A Subject Areas or Table S (your Software Development). In the BSc/BAdvSt, the requirements include a major (48 cp) or program from Science Table A and a second major (48 cp) from Table A or Table S.

So under the BA/BAdvSt, I had Philosophy (Table A) plus Software Development (Table S). Under the BSc/BAdvSt I had Software Development (Table A) plus Philosophy (Table S). These are the same two 48-credit-point majors.

Therefore, I did not have to take any extra units of study to accommodate this switch. This is because I had taken all the mandatory units from the Software Development Unit of Study table, which is the same study table used to progress through a major regardless of if the major is under a Bachelor of Advanced Studies or a Bachelor of Science. The only difference between the two degrees are the core units taken. I completed my core units in an intensive so as to not impede my course progress. 

Upon recieving my offer for the BSc/BAdvSt, the university automatically transferred my previous units completed to the new degree. I applied for this switch on the 8th of May 2023. I was given an offer on the 4th of July 2023, and accepted on the 7th of July 2023. I enrolled on the 18th of July 2023. 

Given this, I did not stop one degree completely and start another seperate degree, the Bachelor of Science and Bachelor of Advanced Studies degree was a continuation of my previous Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Advanced Studies degree, all of my previous study carried over because the necessary units for both degrees (with my majors) are the same. 

I have marked this degree as incomplete because I did not graduate with a degree titled "Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Advanced Studies". More importantly, I wish to be forthcoming to the department as to all of my educational history in Australia. I am unsure if this degree change would render my original degree as 'incomplete', therefore I have chosen to flag it and give background information.
