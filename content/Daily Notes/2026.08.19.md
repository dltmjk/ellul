Hello!
My name is Ahan, I am Ofer's research assistant. Welcome to Sydney (soon), here are some things which I find are worth doing in this city: 

1. The Art Gallery of New South Wales.

Apart from the actual art, they have a wonderful reference library and free weekly screenings of movies. You can find the catalogue here: 
https://www.artgallery.nsw.gov.au/whats-on/cinema/

The sibling MCA (Museum of Contemporary Art) is not worth more than one visit in my opinion. 

2. Bookshops 

The three best bookshops in Sydney are (in my opinion): 
Gould's 
Gleebooks Glebe 
& Unfortunately Kinokuniya (a good selection for new fiction books)

Sappho's is worth an honourable mention, the books are overpriced but it is a neat selection with a good downstairs cafe that is also overpriced. 

3. Community Gardens

Sydney has quite a lot of wonderful community gardens, I find that the communal ones are lovely if you enjoy planting and weeding. The people are very nice and welcoming. USYD has a gardening society that is moving to a new plot.
